Hi! This is future Samikhya speaking.

I had a great time spending my lunches contemplating with friends what animals, colors, or general things we'd be! So much so that decided to create a website dedicated to help you (yes, YOU who's reading this) what household item you are through a short personality test. 

This may be a pretty silly idea for a website, but I had a great time experimenting with features like audio and randomization! Plus, coming up with the simplistic layout was fun too. I tried to use colors that remind me of being at home during summer to create a feeling of familiarity or comfort... did it work? Did I articulate the vibe correctly?

Some smaller details you can look out for: 
- The error message's image changes randomly every time you refresh the screen
- The stripes in the background move slowly overtime (this may be a bit hard to see)
- There's a marquee at the bottom of the site
- The household item spins when you get your result!
